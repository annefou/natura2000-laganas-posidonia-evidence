# figures/

Commit the image that represents your replication here.

One of these images becomes the illustration on your replication's **story page**
— the readable, blog-style page the Science Live platform generates from your
published FORRT chain. Which one is picked:

- a filename containing `main`, `result`, `headline` or `hero` wins;
- otherwise the alphabetically first image in this directory;
- `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp` and `.svg` all count.

So `main_result.png` is the safe name when you have several.

## It has to be committed

This is the part that catches people out. Analysis scripts usually write their
plots to `results/`, which this repo git-ignores along with the other run
artefacts — so the figure exists on the machine that ran the experiment, the
author sees it every day, and the published story page has no image at all.

`results/` is for run output. `figures/` is for the curated figure you want
people to see. Copy or save it here, and commit it.

`scripts/build_chain_draft.py` prints a warning when this directory holds no
image, so you find out while you can still fix it rather than after publishing.

## What makes a good one

The story page is read by people deciding whether your replication is worth
their attention, so the figure should answer the question the replication asked
— typically your result against the original's, with the uncertainty that
decides whether they agree. A figure that shows only your own numbers makes the
reader do the comparison themselves.

## Curated figures of this study (DRAFT, not reviewed)

Each figure is regenerated by a pixi task; files here are copies of the run of 22 October 2026.

| Folder | Figures | Produced by | Inputs and licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `accuracy/` | reference vs Sentinel-2 map, confusion matrix, accuracy by depth | `pixi run accuracy` | HCMR map GR000019 and 2015 survey (CC BY 4.0), Sentinel-2 (Copernicus terms) |
| `climate/` | fig1 observed series, fig2 exposure per HEALPix cell, fig3 Climate DT projection vs observed, fig4 fixed vs detrended heatwaves and degree-days | `pixi run climate-observed`, `climate-dt`, `climate-extended` | Copernicus Marine (Copernicus Marine licence); DestinE Climate DT: derived indicators only |
| `genetics/` | genetic diversity x heat exposure screening, 18 meadows | `pixi run genetics` | Litsi-Mizan et al. 2024 (CC BY 4.0), Copernicus Marine |

`climate/results_reference_check.json` records the comparison of `pipeline/climate/mhw.py` with the reference
implementation of Hobday et al. (2016) (day-by-day agreement 100 % for sea-surface temperature, 99.94 % at meadow depth).

**Sensitivity to the heatwave definition** (`pipeline/climate/10_sensitivity.py`, run by `pixi run climate-extended`):
`climate/results_sensitivity.json` and `climate/sensitivity_laganas.csv` give, for 12 definitions (original baselines
or WMO 1991–2020; 90th, 95th or 99th percentile; fixed or detrended), the shallow Laganas meadows' heatwave days
before and after 2016, the 2024 value and rank, and the trend; `genetics/sensitivity_genetics.csv` lists the meadows
flagged as high exposure and low genetic diversity under each definition.
