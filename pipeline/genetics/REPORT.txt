2. WHAT THE PROTOTYPE USES
--------------------------
Genetics: Litsi-Mizan V, Garcia-Escudero CA, Tsigenopoulos CS, Tsiaras K, Gerakaris V, Apostolaki ET (2024) Unravelling the
genetic pattern of seagrass (Posidonia oceanica) meadows in the Eastern Mediterranean Sea. Biodivers Conserv 33:257-280,
doi:10.1007/s10531-023-02746-0 (University of Crete / HCMR). CC BY 4.0. 18 meadows (Ionian 2, N Aegean 4, S Aegean 8,
Crete 4), sampled 2014-2021 by SCUBA at 4-15 m, 15-31 ramets per meadow (442 in total), 12 microsatellites.
 - ESM1 (xlsx): 334 multilocus genotypes (MLGs) kept after clone removal (GenClone 2.0, psex_FIS < 0.01), 12 loci.
 - ESM2 (docx) Table S1: coordinates (2 decimals, ~1 km) and one sampling depth per meadow. No per-site sampling year.
 - Article Table 1: N ramets per meadow, transcribed to pipeline/genetics/litsi_mizan_2024_table1.csv. Checked: MLGs per
   meadow equal the rows per meadow in ESM1 (all 18), sum N = 442 as in the text.
Recomputed from ESM1 on the authors' 10 loci (01_genetics.py): R = (G-1)/(N-1), Hs (Nei 1987), Ho, NA, rarefied Ar.
Agreement with the published Table 1: G exact; R max |diff| 0.05 (rounding); Hs max |diff| 0.03 (r = 0.98);
Ho max 0.07; NA max 0.4; FIS max 0.47 (r = 0.77). NA/Ho/FIS differences are not resolved (the published values may use
another locus set or all ramets); FIS is not used. R and Hs, the two metrics used, reproduce.
Sensitivity metric R_ident: ESM1 keeps some identical 12-locus genotypes as distinct genets (psex not significant).
Collapsing them lowers R strongly at TRI (0.74 -> 0.30), SOU (1.00 -> 0.57), LIM (0.54 -> 0.35), LES (0.33 -> 0.15),
AFY (0.45 -> 0.34). Clonal richness in low-diversity meadows depends on that clone-assignment choice.

Exposure (02/03): Copernicus Marine, same products and conventions as pipeline/climate/:
 - SST L4 REP 0.05 deg (cmems_SST_MED_SST_L4_REP_OBSERVATIONS_010_021), 1982-01-01..2026-09-22, MHW baseline 1983-2012.
 - Med physics reanalysis 1/24 deg (cmems_mod_med_phy-temp_my_4.2km_P1D-m), thetao at the model level nearest the
   published sampling depth, 1987-01-01..2026-09-30, MHW baseline 1987-2016.
 - Hobday et al. 2016 MHW definition (pipeline/climate/mhw.py); days > 28 C (field/synthesis limit used in
   pipeline/climate) and > 28.9 C (lab LT50, Savva et al. 2018). Means 2016-2025; trends 1982/1987-2025 (Theil-Sen,
   OLS with Newey-West CI); 2026 partial and excluded.
 - Grid: nearest product cell; when it is land at that depth, nearest valid sea cell (flagged). SST: 2 of 18 meadows
   fall back (max 5.4 km). Reanalysis: 10 of 18 (max 10.5 km, LES; LIM 9.0 km, model depth there 7 m).
 - HEALPix: each meadow carries its healpix-geo cell, WGS84 ellipsoid, NESTED, depth 12 (~1.5 km), matching the
   coordinate precision. A finer depth would claim precision the coordinates do not have.

3. RESULTS (work/genetics/tables/meadow_vulnerability.csv, figures/fig_genetic_vulnerability_map.png)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exposure at sampling depth (reanalysis), 18 meadows:
 - MHW days/yr: median 26 in 1987-2015 -> 120 in 2016-2025 (range 87-155). Note: fixed 1987-2016 baseline, so this
   includes the warming trend, by design (Hobday fixed-baseline convention).
 - Trend of MHW days 1987-2025: +20 to +39 days/yr per decade (median +29); 95 % CI excludes 0 at all 18.
   SST: +15 to +33 (median +21), CI excludes 0 at all 18.
 - 2024 was the record MHW-days year at all 18 meadows, at depth and at the surface (median 277 MHW days at depth).
   2024 was the record annual maximum at depth at 7 of 18, at the surface at 4 of 18.
 - Days > 28 C at sampling depth (2016-2025 mean): > 1 day/yr only at AFY (11.8), TRI (9.8), SOU (1.6); zero or < 1
   elsewhere. At the surface (SST): AFY 14.9, TRI 8.4, PAR 5.5, KEF 4.2. The two shallow (5 m) North Aegean gulf sites (AFY, Toroneos
   Gulf; TRI, mouth of the Pagasitikos Gulf) are the hot spots.
Genetics: R 0.33-1.00 (R_ident 0.15-0.95), Hs 0.24-0.46. Lowest diversity: LES, LIM (N Aegean), then NIS, TRI.
No monotonic relation between genetic diversity and exposure across the 18 (Spearman R vs MHW days -0.07,
Hs vs MHW days +0.42, n = 18), so the two axes carry different information.

Classification (rules fixed in 04_classify.py before results were seen; relative to these 18 meadows only):
 E = mean percentile rank of (MHW days/yr 2016-2025 at depth, days > 28 C/yr 2016-2025 at depth);
 G = mean percentile rank of (R, Hs); high exposure = 6 highest E; low diversity = 6 lowest G.
 Class 1 high exposure + low diversity: AFY (Afyto), TRI (Trikeri), NIS (Nisyros)
 Class 2 high exposure only:            PAR, SOU, GOU
 Class 3 low diversity only:            LES, LIM, SAN
 Class 4 neither:                       9 meadows (Cyclades except SAN, Crete except GOU, KEF, STR)
 Robustness: AFY and TRI stay in class 1 under all four alternatives (R_ident; SST exposure; MHW days only; days > 28 C
 only). NIS stays in only 1 of 4 (it drops out with SST, or either exposure metric alone). SOU enters with R_ident.
 Read: AFY and TRI are the robust candidates; NIS is marginal.
This is a co-occurrence screening. It does not show that low diversity makes a meadow more sensitive to heat, nor that
any meadow is harmed. The link "low genetic/clonal diversity -> lower resilience" is a hypothesis: shown experimentally in another seagrass
(Zostera marina after the 2003 heatwave: Reusch et al. 2005 PNAS 102:2826, doi:10.1073/pnas.0500008102), not for heat
in Posidonia. The only Posidonia-wide test (Jahnke et al. 2015, 56 meadows) found weak POSITIVE correlations between
genetic diversity and human disturbance, which the authors read as local loss of low-diversity meadows and a temporal
mismatch due to clone longevity: diversity metrics may lag decline by decades.

4. LIMITS
---------
 - One study, one marker set: all 18 meadows come from Litsi-Mizan et al. 2024 (10 SSR loci), so the metrics are
   comparable among them. They are NOT directly comparable with other studies (different loci, sample sizes, clone
   criteria, Ar rarefaction size); merging Italian or Med-wide data needs common loci re-scoring or a standardised
   panel (allele-size binning differs between labs).
 - Neutral microsatellites measure diversity and clonality, not thermal adaptation. One locus (Pooc-PC045G11F) is a
   Bayescan outlier and excluded; no SNP / genome-wide data for Greek meadows are open.
 - Sampling years 2014-2021, not given per meadow; genetic composition may have changed (e.g. after 2021-2024 MHWs).
 - Sampling depth is a single value per meadow; the meadow's depth range and the depth of the deep edge are unknown.
   The reanalysis is 4.2 km and its coast is coarse: 10 of 18 meadows use a neighbouring sea cell (up to 10.5 km) whose
   model column is deeper or shallower than the real site; temperature at 5 m in a 4 km cell is not the temperature
   in a sheltered bay (e.g. LIM model depth 7 m). Satellite SST (skin/foundation) is given alongside for comparison.
 - Coordinates have 2 decimals (~1 km); HEALPix depth 12 is the finest honest cell.
 - No condition data (shoot density, mortality, flowering, necrosis) enter: the classification is exposure x diversity,
   not impact. The ranks are relative; with 18 meadows a different cut (quartiles, absolute thresholds) changes members.
 - Ne is not estimated (10 SSR loci on 10-27 genets per meadow is too little for LD-Ne).
