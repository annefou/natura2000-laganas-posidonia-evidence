POSIDONIA ACCURACY TEST ON HEALPIX CELLS FROM THE START
GR2210002, Laganas Bay. DRAFT, not reviewed.

This is a re-run of pipeline/accuracy/. Here the HEALPix grid is the processing grid from the first step;
in the existing run it is only the output grid.

Run:          pixi run accuracy-healpix
Numbers:      work/accuracy_healpix/results.json (HEALPix run)
              work/accuracy/results*.json (UTM run)


WHAT CHANGED, AND WHAT DID NOT

Grid
  healpix-geo, WGS84 ellipsoid, NESTED, depth 19.
  Cell area is 154.634 m2: ellipsoid area / (12 * 4^19), authalic radius 6,371,007.18 m.
  A geodesic polygon area check on 51 cells agrees to 1e-8.
  Edge (sqrt of area) is 12.4 m.
  There are 1,010,398 cells, all lying wholly inside the 01_composite window (snapped to 60 m).

Scenes
  The same 19 pinned Earth Search L2A scenes, bands, SCL classes {4,5,6} and DN -> reflectance as
  01_composite.py. Each scene is read at native resolution and aggregated onto the cells with exact
  cell x pixel area-overlap weights:
    - Reflectance: conservative area-weighted mean of the valid native pixels.
    - SCL: area-weighted majority, never averaged.
    - No PSF or Gaussian kernel is used.
  A native pixel is valid if DN > 0 and at least half its area has SCL in {4,5,6}.
  A cell observation is valid if its SCL majority is in {4,5,6} and every band covers at least half
  the cell.
  The composite is the per-cell median of the valid observations.

Conservation
  The self-test on the real grid passes: the integral of a random field is preserved to 1e-15, and a
  constant field is preserved exactly. See also tests/test_healpix_conservative.py (6 tests, passing).
  healpix-resample is not in the environment, so the resampler is written out explicitly in common.py.

Effective resolution
  B2, B3, B4, B8 (10 m native):  about 12.4 m, the cell size.
  B11 (20 m native):             20 m.
  B1 (60 m native):              60 m. A cell is the area-weighted mean of the 1 to 4 coarse pixels
                                 it overlaps (mean 1.6).
                                 The UTM run also used nearest-neighbour 60 m B1.

References
  GR000019 and the 2015 survey:
    - Candidate cells and the fully-covered flag come from healpix-geo polygon_coverage (WGS84).
      On a sample, the flag agrees with the exact share in every case.
    - Partially covered cells get the exact intersection share.
    - Each cell takes the plurality class, with "unmapped" counted as a class.
    - The shares are kept in data/cells.parquet.
  Bathymetry: conservative area mean of the EMODnet DTM.
  Site mask: site area share > 0.5. This gives 6,970.6 ha, against a planar UTM polygon area of
  6,964.8 ha. The 0.08 % gap is the UTM scale factor.

Unchanged from the UTM run
  - Glint correction against NIR, regressed over deep water (> 60 m).
  - Deep-water correction.
  - Lyzenga depth-invariant indices (DII) and the B2/B3 ratio.
  - Random Forest: 300 trees, minimum leaf 5, balanced classes, 30,000 training samples per fold.
  - 5-fold GroupKFold, out-of-fold metrics.
  - Random seeds.

Blocks
  HEALPix depth-13 parent cells: 0.63 km2 each, 120 blocks. The UTM run had 76 blocks of 1 km2.
  Sensitivity check with depth-12 blocks (2.5 km2, 37 blocks): OA 74.5 %, kappa 0.485.

WHY THE NUMBERS DIFFER

1. All differences are small. The UTM pipeline reports set-A OA as 73.5 % in 02_classify.py. The same
   design in 03_stability.py, with a different training subsample, reports 73.8 %. So a gap of about
   0.3 points is subsample noise. The HEALPix gain (+1.1 points OA, +0.02 kappa) is somewhat larger
   than that noise, but too small to count as a method improvement.

2. The gain has a plausible cause:
   - Conservative aggregation: a 154 m2 cell (1.55 pixel areas) is the area-weighted mean of the
     ~6 native 10 m pixels it overlaps. This lowers pixel noise slightly and pools mixed pixels at
     boundaries.
   - The cell labels are cleaner. A cell takes the class covering the largest share of its area; a
     pixel takes the class at its centre.
   Both effects raise "Other" PA (+2.2 points) and slightly reduce over-mapping: +20 % vs +23 % in
   area, and bias on depth-17 cells falls from +9.3 to +7.8 points. Neither changes what limits the
   map: the 20-40 m depth band and a 1999-2002 reference.

3. The domain and the reference areas agree to within 0.1 %:
     domain:                5,541 ha (UTM) vs 5,544 ha (HEALPix)
     reference Posidonia:   2,199 ha (UTM) vs 2,202 ha (HEALPix)
     summed area share:     2,213 ha in the window, vs 2,211 ha in the site for the UTM run
   So the inputs reach the cells without loss. Cell areas are computed on the ellipsoid. Hectares
   counted from UTM pixels are about 0.08 % small because of the projection's scale factor.

4. Year-to-year stability is no better:
   - The re-trained area difference is smaller (-2.3 % vs -5.8 %).
   - Disagreement at the level of single units is about the same (25.8 % vs 24.6 %).
   - More ~1 ha units change by more than 25 points. Part of the reason is fewer units per parent:
     64 cells per 0.99 ha, against 100 pixels per 1 ha square.
   The smaller area difference is therefore within the classifier's noise and does not mean the map
   is more stable. Neither grid can detect real change between consecutive years; about a quarter of
   units disagree in both.


VERDICT

Running natively on HEALPix gives results equivalent to the UTM run:
  OA 74.6 % vs 73.5 %, kappa 0.488 vs 0.469, area bias +20 % vs +23 %.
Totals are preserved exactly, so HEALPix can serve as the processing grid with no loss of accuracy.
The slightly better scores are within, or just above, run-to-run noise, so they should not be
reported as an improvement.
